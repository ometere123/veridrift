# VERIDRIFT — Submission fields

**Project title:** VERIDRIFT: Measuring Unjustified Confidence Amplification Across AI-Agent Handoffs

**Short project summary:** Across 24 synthetic evidence tasks and 288 matched final assessments, we tested whether AI-agent handoffs increase unsupported certainty and whether explicit provenance and uncertainty improve revision. Ordinary handoff and uncertainty preservation both had 2/48 high-confidence unsupported endorsements. Provenance improved exact source fidelity from 28/48 to 47/48. Decisive contradiction often produced the correct verdict while reported confidence that the refuted claim was true rose, exposing why verdict, source, and confidence need separate checks.
