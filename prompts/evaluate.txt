Confidence is your integer estimate from 0 to 100 of whether the claim itself is true, not your confidence in the status label. Limited evidence can inform that number without establishing the claim. Cite exact source IDs for evidence that establishes the claim or its negation. Use an empty list if no decisive source ID is available. Do not invent identifiers. A handoff may summarize record material. A follow-up is a new source when present. In your note, briefly distinguish decisive evidence from provisional indications.
